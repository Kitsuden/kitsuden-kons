# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.

To report unacceptable behaviour, open a
[new issue](https://github.com/Kitsuden/kitsuden-kons/issues/new) whose title is only
"Conduct contact requested" and that says nothing else: no names, no links, no account of what
happened. A maintainer ([@vigogami](https://github.com/vigogami)) answers there with a private
channel, and you send the report through it. Reports are handled confidentially.
